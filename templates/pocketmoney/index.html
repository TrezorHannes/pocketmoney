About PocketMoney

PocketMoney automates recurring pocket money, contractor payroll, and monthly server bills.

Key Highlights
  • Universal Recipient: Accepts internal LNbits Wallet IDs, Lightning Addresses (alice@getalby.com), and LNURL-pay.
  • Zero Fees Internally: Internal LNbits transfers settle instantly with 0 routing fees without needing child invoice keys!
  • All-or-Nothing Batching: If wallet balance is insufficient, payments are skipped safely with an alert rather than paying partially.
  • Multi-Currency: Send in EUR, USD, GBP, or sats with just-in-time exchange rate conversion.
